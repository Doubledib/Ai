Settings

Before you sleep

Plug the phone in and leave this page open with the screen on. Use “Dim screen” so it stays dark. If you lock the phone or switch apps, iOS turns the microphone off and listening pauses until you come back.

Sound is checked on this phone and thrown away unless something is heard. Recordings and transcripts stay on this phone only.

Recordings

The first time, this downloads a speech model (about 40 MB for English, 80 MB for other languages). After that it runs on this phone.
